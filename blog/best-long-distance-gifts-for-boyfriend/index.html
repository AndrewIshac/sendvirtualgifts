The best long-distance gifts for your boyfriend

By the SendLove team · October 12, 2026

Quick answer

The best long-distance gifts for a boyfriend are a countdown to your next visit, a planned date for when you reunite, a photo puzzle from your last trip, a short list of reasons you love him, a touch bracelet, or a piece of your clothing that smells like you.

Being far from your boyfriend is hard on both of you, even if he's not the type to say it. These gifts help him feel you're around, and a few give him something to count down to.

Quick disclosure: this site is run by the people behind SendLove, which is one of the sites on this list. We've tried to be fair about where the others do a better job, and we say so when they do.

What guys in long-distance relationships tend to value

A lot of men in long-distance relationships won't tell you they're struggling. But the things they appreciate tend to be practical and quiet: knowing when the next visit is, feeling like you're thinking about them during the day, and having something that's a bit of you in their space.

Gifts you can send him today

1. A countdown to your next visit

If you only send one gift, make it this. The day you book tickets, send him a countdown. Guys often check these way more than they'll admit.

2. A date planned for the first night you're back

Plan the evening: where you'll eat, what you'll do after. He picks the time from the gift. It takes the planning off him and makes the reunion feel real.

3. A puzzle with a photo from your last visit

Do it together on a call. He solves it, you watch his face when the photo appears.

4. A "why I love you" list

Keep each reason short. Mix serious ("you never make me feel like a burden") with funny ("you can't parallel park and refuse to admit it").

5. A playful question

"Will you call me tonight even though you have work tomorrow?" with a No button that runs away. Small, silly, and it'll make his day.

You can make all five on SendLove.

Gifts worth shipping to him

6. A hoodie or T-shirt you've worn

It sounds too simple. It isn't. Spray a bit of your perfume on it before you send it.

7. A touch bracelet

You tap yours, his buzzes. Even guys who don't usually wear jewelry often like these, because it's less about the bracelet and more about the tap.

8. A care package from your city

Snacks from your favourite shop, a coffee you both love, a handwritten note. Small things from your world.

9. Something for your calls

A decent phone stand, a ring light, or the same game you can play together online. Anything that makes your daily calls a bit better is a gift you both use.

What to write with it

Keep it short and honest. Something like:

"I miss you more than I say. This is so you have something to look at until I see you. Not long now."

That's enough. He'll read it more than once.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good long-distance gift for a boyfriend?

A countdown to your next visit, a planned date for your reunion, a photo puzzle, a worn hoodie, or a touch bracelet.

What can I send my long-distance boyfriend online?

A virtual gift like a countdown, date plan, puzzle or list of reasons you love him, sent as a link.

How do I make my long-distance boyfriend feel loved?

Small, regular gestures help most: a surprise gift on a normal day, planning your next visit, and telling him specifically what you appreciate.
