The best long-distance gifts for your girlfriend

By the SendLove team · October 12, 2026

Quick answer

The best long-distance gifts for a girlfriend are a personal love letter, virtual flowers timed to arrive when she wakes up, a photo album of your relationship, a countdown to your next visit, a planned reunion date, or a physical keepsake like a connected lamp or something that smells like you.

When your girlfriend is miles away, the small things matter more: a good-morning message, a call that runs late, a gift on a day she didn't expect one. Here are the gifts that do the most to close that gap.

Quick disclosure: this site is run by the people behind SendLove, which is one of the sites on this list. We've tried to be fair about where the others do a better job, and we say so when they do.

The idea behind a good long-distance gift for her

It's not about the size of the gift. It's about showing her she's on your mind when she can't see you. The best long-distance gifts do that in a way she can come back to: something to reread, to look at, to count down with.

Gifts you can send her right now

1. A love letter for her to keep

Write the things you'd say if she were sitting next to you. Start with a memory, tell her something you've never said, end with something you can't wait to do together. She'll reread it on the nights she misses you most.

2. Flowers that land when she wakes up

Check the time difference and send virtual flowers so they're waiting when she opens her phone in the morning. It's a small thing that shows you know her day, not just her address.

3. Your relationship as a photo album

From your first photo to your last goodbye at the airport. Add captions. For a lot of long-distance couples, seeing the whole story at once is the reminder that the distance is temporary.

4. A countdown to the next time you see her

Send it the day you book. She'll check it more than you think.

5. A date she can say yes to

Plan the first evening after you reunite and let her pick the time. She'll spend the weeks before looking forward to it.

6. A "why I love you" list

Ten or fifteen reasons, specific and a little silly. She'll screenshot it.

All six can be made on SendLove, which is made for couples. The love letter is free.

Gifts worth shipping to her

7. A connected lamp

Touch yours and hers lights up. It's a gentle way to say "thinking of you" without sending a text at 3am.

8. Your hoodie

Worn, not washed. Ask anyone who's done long distance. This is the gift.

9. A print of your two cities

A map or a print with both cities on it, and the distance between them. A reminder that you're connected, and something nice for her wall.

10. "Open when" letters

A small bundle of envelopes: open when you miss me, open when you can't sleep, open when you had a bad day. Write them by hand if you can. You can also send them as virtual letters, one at a time, when she needs them.

One thing that makes any of these better

Open it together. Send the gift, then call her while she opens it. Watching her react is half the point, and it turns a gift into a moment you shared, even from far away.
