The best virtual gift websites for your boyfriend

By the SendLove team · October 12, 2026

Quick answer

A photo puzzle, a date plan he can accept, or a short list of reasons you love him. Interactive gifts tend to land better with guys than long messages.

Most gift advice for guys is a list of gadgets. This isn't that. If you want to send him something online that actually lands, here's where to make it and what to send.

Quick disclosure: this site is run by the people behind SendLove, which is one of the sites on this list. We've tried to be fair about where the others do a better job, and we say so when they do.

Why a virtual gift works well for a lot of guys

Here's something a lot of people don't say out loud: many men rarely get told, in detail, why they're appreciated. They get "love you" texts, sure. They don't often get a whole page about it.

That's why a virtual gift can hit harder than you'd expect. It's not about the object. It's about the fact that you sat down and made something just for him. And unlike a card, he can open it again on a bad day.

What to send him, by situation

For his birthday

Go with a photo album of the past year, or a puzzle that ends on your favourite photo of him. Add one line about something he did this year that you were proud of. Not a big speech, one specific thing.

After a fight

Skip the jokes. A short letter works best: what you're sorry for, what matters more to you than being right, and something you want to do together soon. Then actually call him.

When you're long distance

A countdown to your next visit gives him something to look at every day. Pair it with a date plan for the first night you're back together.

Just because

Flowers might seem like an odd choice for a guy, but plenty of men have never received flowers in their life. Virtual or not, it's a gesture people remember.

A few things that make it better

  • Use the name you actually call him, not his full name.
  • Pick a photo where he looks happy, not one where he looks good. Those aren't the same.
  • Send it with a short text so it doesn't look like spam: "Open this when you have a minute."
  • Don't over-explain. If there's an inside joke, let it be an inside joke.
