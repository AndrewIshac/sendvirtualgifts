The best virtual gifts for your girlfriend

By the SendLove team · October 12, 2026

Quick answer

A personal love letter or a 'why I love you' list, because they're about her specifically. Flowers on an ordinary day and a photo album of your relationship are close behind.

She doesn't need another "thinking of you 😘" text. She'd love something that shows you actually thought about it. These are the virtual gifts that do that, and where to make each one.

Quick disclosure: this site is run by the people behind SendLove, which is one of the sites on this list. We've tried to be fair about where the others do a better job, and we say so when they do.

What she'll actually care about

Let's be real for a second. Most people don't care how fancy a gift's animation is. What they care about is whether it feels like it came from you. So the best virtual gift for your girlfriend is one with your memories in it: a moment you shared, a joke only she gets, a photo she forgot you took.

Keep that in mind and almost any of these will work.

The 8 best virtual gifts for her

1. A love letter that opens like a real envelope

This is the classic for a reason. Write the things you think but never say: the moment you knew, the small things she does that you notice, what you're looking forward to. Three honest paragraphs are plenty.

Where to make it: SendLove's Love Letter is free. AIskyla and SendVirtualGift also have letter templates.

2. A "why I love you" list

Easier to write than a letter, and somehow more fun to read. Mix big reasons with tiny ones. "You're the kindest person I know" next to "you dance while you brush your teeth" is exactly right.

Where to make it: SendLove's Why I Love You.

3. Flowers for no reason

Flowers on Valentine's Day are expected. Flowers on a random Wednesday after she had a long day? That's the one she'll remember.

Where to make it: DigiFlower if you want to build the bouquet stem by stem; SendLove's Virtual Flowers if you want to send other gifts from the same place.

4. A photo album of your relationship

Start from your first photo together and work forward. Add a line under some of them about what was happening. You'll probably enjoy making this one as much as she enjoys opening it.

Where to make it: SendLove's Photo Album, or GiftFeels' digital scrapbook.

5. A puzzle hiding your favourite photo of her

Playful and short. She solves it, and the reveal is a photo of her laughing, or of the two of you somewhere she loved.

Where to make it: SendLove's Puzzle Gift or AIskyla's jigsaw.

6. A date she can say yes to

Plan the whole thing (the place, the vibe) and let her pick the day and time from the gift. It takes the planning off her plate, which a lot of people find more romantic than anything.

Where to make it: SendLove's Plan a Date.

7. A countdown to seeing each other

For long-distance couples especially. Every time she opens it, the number gets smaller.

Where to make it: SendLove's Countdown to Reunion.

8. A shared 3D room for a big occasion

If it's her birthday and you can't be there, GiftVerse's 3D room lets you blow out a virtual cake together on a call. It's a bigger purchase, so save it for a big day.

How to send it so it lands

  • Time it. First thing in the morning, or right after something stressful she had to do.
  • Add a short heads-up. "I made you something, open it when you're alone 🙂" builds a bit of anticipation.
  • Be specific. "I love your smile" is nice. "I love how you smile before you tell a story because you already know the ending is funny" is the one she'll reread.
