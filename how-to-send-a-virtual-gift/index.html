How to send a virtual gift online

Sending a virtual gift takes a few minutes. Here's the full process, plus tips to make sure it lands at the perfect moment.

Step 1: Choose the right kind of gift

Think about the moment. A love letter fits when you want to say something meaningful. Flowers fit a quick "thinking of you." A photo puzzle or album fits when you want to share memories. A date plan fits when you want something to look forward to.

Step 2: Personalize it

Add your message and photos. This is the part that makes the gift theirs. Mention a real memory, use their nickname, and choose pictures that tell a story.

Step 3: Get your gift link

Once your gift is ready, you get a unique link. That link is the gift. Anyone who opens it sees what you made, so send it only to the person it's for.

Step 4: Share it your way

  • Text message or WhatsApp: the most direct way; it shows up right in your chat.
  • Instagram or Messenger DM: great if that's where you usually talk.
  • Email: nice for a more formal or longer message.
  • QR code: print the link as a QR code inside a card for a real-world surprise.

Step 5: Pick your moment

Timing turns a nice gift into a memorable one. Send it when they wake up, before something important, or while you're on a call so you can see their reaction.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Sending it without a short message telling them to open it.
  • Using generic text you'd write to anyone.
  • Forgetting their time zone.

Frequently asked questions

Does the receiver need an account or an app?

No. They just tap the link and the gift opens in their browser.

Can I send a virtual gift from my phone?

Yes. You can create and send it entirely from your phone.

Can I send the same gift link to several people?

It's best to make a separate gift for each person so each one feels personal.
