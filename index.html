What is a virtual gift?

A virtual gift is a surprise you make online and send as a link. Instead of a box in the mail, the person you love opens an interactive moment on their phone: your words, your photos, little animations, sometimes a game or a question only they can answer.

It isn't a gift card or a code. It's closer to a handmade card, only it can move, play music and reach someone on the other side of the planet instantly.

Why people choose virtual gifts

No shipping, no waiting. It lands the moment you hit send, even across oceans and time zones.

Made by you. Every gift carries your own message and photos, so it never feels generic.

Easy to open. One tap on a link. No app to install, no account for the person receiving it.

Virtual gift questions

What is a virtual gift?

A virtual gift is a personal surprise made online, like an animated love letter, virtual flowers or a photo album, that you send as a link. The person opens it instantly on their phone or computer.

How do I send a virtual gift?

Choose a gift on SendLove, personalize it with your words and photos, then share the link by text, WhatsApp, Instagram or email.

Does the person need an app to open it?

No. A virtual gift opens in the web browser from the link you send, on any phone or computer.

Are virtual gifts good for long-distance relationships?

Yes. A virtual gift arrives the moment you send it, with no shipping, customs or time zone delays, which makes it one of the easiest ways to surprise someone far away.
