Send virtual flowers online

Real flowers are lovely, but they're not always possible: they're far away, it's late, or you just want to say something small today. Virtual flowers arrive in seconds and come with your own message.

What are virtual flowers?

Virtual flowers are a digital bouquet you send as a link. When the person opens it, the flowers appear on their screen along with the note you wrote. They're a gentle, quick way to show you care, and unlike a real bouquet, they never wilt.

When virtual flowers are perfect

  • You live in different cities or countries.
  • You want to say sorry and start the conversation softly.
  • You want to congratulate someone the moment you hear the news.
  • You want to send something kind on a hard day.
  • You just thought of them and want them to know.

What to write with your flowers

Keep the note short and specific. Some starting points:

  • "Saw these and thought of you."
  • "I know today is a lot. I'm proud of you."
  • "I'm sorry about earlier. Can we talk tonight?"
  • "No reason. Just that I love you."

Virtual flowers vs real flowers

They aren't rivals. Real flowers fill a room; virtual flowers fill a moment. Many people send virtual flowers between visits and save real bouquets for when they can hand them over in person.

Frequently asked questions

How do I send virtual flowers?

Choose the flowers gift on SendLove, write your message, and share the link by text, WhatsApp, Instagram or email.

Can I send virtual flowers internationally?

Yes. Since they're sent as a link, virtual flowers reach anyone with an internet connection, anywhere.

Do virtual flowers include a message?

Yes, you add your own personal note that appears with the bouquet.
