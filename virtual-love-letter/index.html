How to write and send a virtual love letter

Texts disappear in a scroll. A love letter is something they keep. A virtual love letter brings back the feeling of opening an envelope, and it reaches them the moment you send it.

Why write a love letter today?

We send dozens of messages a day, but rarely sit down and say what we really feel. A letter slows things down. It shows you took time, chose your words, and meant them. Sending it as a virtual love letter keeps that feeling while making sure it arrives right away.

A simple structure that works

  1. Start with a memory. "I still think about the night we got lost looking for that restaurant…"
  2. Say what they mean to you. Be specific: the way they listen, how they make hard days lighter.
  3. Share something you've never said. A thought you've kept to yourself, a moment you knew.
  4. Look ahead. End with something you can't wait to do together.

Tips for writing it

  • Write like you talk. They love your voice, not a poet's.
  • Short is fine. A few honest paragraphs beat a page of clichés.
  • Read it out loud once before sending.
  • Add a photo that goes with your favourite memory.

Love letter ideas for different moments

  • Long distance: describe the first thing you'll do when you see them.
  • Anniversary: write about how you've changed together.
  • After an argument: lead with what matters more than being right.
  • Just because: list small things you noticed about them this week.

Frequently asked questions

What is a virtual love letter?

It's a love letter you write online and send as a link. It opens on their screen like an envelope, with your words and optional photos inside.

How long should a love letter be?

There's no rule. Three or four sincere paragraphs are usually perfect.

Can I add photos to my love letter?

Yes, adding a photo that matches a memory in the letter makes it even more personal.
